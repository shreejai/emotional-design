Apple's Approach to Layering Scroll Transitions

Apple’s layered scroll transitions create a cinematic experience by animating different elements at varying speeds and delays. This approach, inspired by parallax motion, establishes a hierarchy of attention, guiding users through the content in a natural and engaging way. By implementing this technique, developers can elevate their scroll pages from flat to immersive, leaving a lasting impression on users.

It's simpler than you think, but the execution is everything.

The Apple Layering Formula

  • Background sets the mood (subtle color shifts, gradients) -> ≈ 0ms
  • Headline establishes context (fades in first, slower movement) -> ≈ 100ms delay
  • Hero content commands attention (main product, faster/dramatic movement) -> ≈ 300ms delay
  • Details complete the story (specs, features, appear last) -> ≈ 600ms delay
  1. Layer 1 is the background, to set the mood with gradients and colors.
  2. Layer 2 is the headline, to introduce the concept.
  3. Layer 3 is the hero, which commands your attention. And this is your main product or the image.
  4. And the Layer 4 is the details, that complete the story.
